What Makes This Work

Not just ads — a full execution framework built for plumbing lead generation.

Dedicated Landing Pages

Each campaign gets its own page. Emergency gets a call-now page. Water heater gets a water heater page. No sending traffic to the homepage.

Call Tracking on Every Ad

Phone calls are the primary conversion for plumbing. Every number gets tracked so we know exactly which campaigns and keywords drive revenue.

Smart Ad Scheduling

Emergency campaign runs 24/7. Service campaigns front-loaded Mon–Sat 7am–7pm when homeowners are actively searching.

Negative Keywords from Day 1

Block "DIY," "how to," "tools for," "free" immediately. Tommie's already ranks organically for informational content — we don't pay for those clicks.

Responsive Search Ads

10–15 headlines tested per ad group. Let the algorithm optimize to the winners — then scale what works.
